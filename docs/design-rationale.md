# NivaasCare — UX & Conversion Rationale

## Brief
Future Interns UI/UX Task 1 is a local service business website redesign focused on lead generation and conversion. This concept includes a homepage, service page and contact/lead page.

## Concept and assumptions
NivaasCare is a fictional home-cleaning service concept for Delhi NCR. Brand details, contact information, service area and operational statements are sample content and must be verified before any real launch.

## User problem
A potential customer needs to understand what can be booked, what to expect, and how to request an estimate without a lengthy process.

## Key design decisions
1. **Value proposition first:** The homepage hero names the service and communicates a practical benefit.
2. **Distinct service choices:** Deep cleaning, kitchen care and bathroom care are separated so visitors can choose the relevant option.
3. **CTAs at decision points:** Estimate links appear in navigation, hero, service sections and closing banner.
4. **Trust without invented proof:** Scope confirmation, flexible slots and local support are used as cues; no fake star ratings, testimonials or credentials are shown.
5. **Focused enquiry form:** Name, phone, email, service and locality are required; the message field is optional.
6. **Mobile-first readability:** Navigation collapses, sections stack and fields use the available width.

## Information architecture
Home → Services → Contact / Request an estimate.

## Proposed success measures
Measure hero CTA click-through, service enquiry clicks, form starts, form completion rate, field-level abandonment and mobile-vs-desktop completion. These are proposed metrics, not measured results.

## Limitations and next steps
The form is a front-end prototype only; it does not send or store data. Before launch, replace sample business information with verified details, confirm service scope and pricing, add a privacy policy and connect a secure form endpoint. Validate the flow with usability testing.
